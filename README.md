# Ship Wheels

Decorative ship's wheels in every wood. Just the wheel: no ship physics, nothing that
turns your build into a boat.

Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3. Needs Fabric API. Install on the server and every client.

- **Craft:** 4 sticks around 1 plank (a plus shape) makes a wheel of that wood. Oak, spruce,
  birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, poplar, bamboo, crimson and
  warped.
- **Place:** click the side of a wall, post or fence and the wheel mounts there with its axle
  into the block. Click a floor and it stands up facing you.
- **Spin:** right-click it. Click the right half to turn it one way, the left half for the
  other. Click again to spin faster; it slows down on its own.
